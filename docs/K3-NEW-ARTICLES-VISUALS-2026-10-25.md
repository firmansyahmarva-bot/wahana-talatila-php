Created 25 October 2026 (Asia/Jakarta). Eight new educational URLs, not rewrites of certification guides. Sources appear in each article. Traffic volumes and rankings are not verified.

## Visual decisions

Eight original editable SVG diagrams with PNG versions are saved in public/images/articles/k3-guides/. They explain APAR media/rating selection, five sign groups, five housekeeping steps, the fire triangle, condition versus event, PASS, conceptual TT protection, and action versus condition. These educational drawings are not certified sign masters or installation drawings.

## Final prompt set

### apar-equipment

Use case: educational editorial product illustration. Create one landscape 3:2 photorealistic image for an Indonesian occupational safety guide comparing portable fire extinguisher equipment. Plain light gray studio backdrop, three unbranded red fire extinguishers standing upright separately with ample space, evenly spaced. LEFT: stored-pressure dry chemical extinguisher with pressure gauge, black flexible hose and small nozzle. CENTER: CO2 extinguisher, no pressure gauge, prominent black flared discharge horn on a black hose, proper insulated grip. RIGHT: stored-pressure water extinguisher, pressure gauge, black hose with a straight nozzle. All units intact with safety pins fitted and handles closed, generic blank white rectangular labels without text. No people, no fire, no instructions, no logos, no text, no arrows, no badges, no certification marks. Realistic proportions and hardware. Wide enough to annotate each of the three columns below later. The image demonstrates physical differences only, never imply identifying extinguisher media by color or shape alone.

### 5r-before-after

Use case: educational editorial workplace illustration. Create one landscape image split into two equally sized side-by-side panels showing exactly the SAME small industrial maintenance workbench before and after workplace organization (5R/5S). Photorealistic natural daylight, same fixed eye-level camera, simple workshop backdrop. LEFT: cluttered bench with mixed hand tools, extra empty boxes and spare parts with no clear storage, a few boxes intruding into the walking aisle. RIGHT: same bench, same tools sensibly arranged on a tool shadow board, spare parts in labeled-looking plain blank bins, unnecessary empty boxes removed, clear walking aisle, designated storage, a small blank check sheet fixed on the bench side. No people, no machines in operation, no exposed electricity, no flames, no chemical containers, no readable text or logos, no decorative labels. Realistic plausible improvements that teach access, organization, and sustaining checks; not a luxury makeover. Maintain the exact same workshop and objects so the comparison is meaningful.
